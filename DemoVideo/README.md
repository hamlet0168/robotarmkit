## AI agent make a phone call to me / AI助理给我打电话
One day I couldn't remember where I'd left my phone, so I asked my AI assistant in OpenClaw for help. She used an API to call the robot arm service, then used her own real phone to dial my number. I heard my phone ringing and found it in the corner of the sofa.
某天我忘记电话放哪里了，于是我求助电脑里OpenClaw中的AI助理，她用她专属的手机给我的手机打了个电话，我听见铃声在沙发角落找到了我的电话。。
